Our Approach

Technology follows the requirement.

MHS does not treat product selection as a standalone exercise. Technology is evaluated in the context of the site, performance needs, integration requirements, maintainability and the wider infrastructure environment.

That approach helps keep systems practical to deploy, easier to support and aligned with the operational objective rather than a single vendor or device.
