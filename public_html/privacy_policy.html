Privacy Policy

How MHS Technologies handles information submitted through this website.

Effective date: November 20, 2025

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how MHS Technologies collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website or contact us about our services.

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry, we may collect your name, company, email address, phone number, project location, service area of interest and project details. We may also receive standard technical information generated by your browser or hosting environment.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to project and service enquiries.
  • To understand requirements and route enquiries to the relevant team.
  • To maintain reasonable business and project communication records.
  • To improve website reliability, content and user experience.

Data Protection

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or misuse. No internet-based system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Cookies

This website may use essential or analytics-related browser technologies where required for functionality and performance measurement. Browser settings can be used to control cookies where applicable.

Third-Party Sharing

MHS Technologies does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with authorized service providers or project partners only where reasonably necessary to respond to an enquiry or deliver an agreed service.

Your Rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information held by MHS Technologies where applicable. Contact us using the email below.
